# Decision Log

> Significant architectural and product decisions, recorded for future reference. Use this to understand *why* the codebase is shaped the way it is.

## Template

```
### [ADR-N] Decision title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Accepted | Superseded by ADR-X | Deprecated
**Context:** Why did this decision need to be made? What were the constraints?
**Decision:** What was decided?
**Consequences:** What are the trade-offs? What becomes easier or harder?
```

---

*Decisions will be added here as the project evolves. Start recording from the first meaningful architectural choice.*
